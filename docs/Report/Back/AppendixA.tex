\chapter{Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence}
\label{generative-ai}

\begingroup
\setlength{\emergencystretch}{3em}
During the preparation of this report, OpenAI Codex and ChatGPT with the GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.6 Sol models were used for language correction, translation, and the simplification of complex sentences. Figure~\ref{fig:concept-interaction-inputs} and the conceptual sketches in Figures~\ref{fig:concept-presentation-templates} and~\ref{fig:concept-placement-areas} were also created with ChatGPT based on content and visual instructions provided by the author.

All generated responses were reviewed and revised where necessary. Technical information was checked against the cited sources, the existing source code, and the results obtained during the project. All decisions regarding the implementation, evaluation, and interpretation of the results were made by the author.

The use of generative AI during development, especially for source code, technical documentation, and illustrations, is documented separately in the repository README under \emph{Use of AI during development}.\footnote{Project repository and full AI declaration: \href{https://github.com/jannick1312/BCs-VR-Museum/blob/main/README.md}{README in the project repository}.}

\endgroup
