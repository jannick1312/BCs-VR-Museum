\chapter{Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence}
\label{generative-ai}

\begingroup
\setlength{\emergencystretch}{3em}
During the preparation of this report, I used OpenAI Codex and ChatGPT with the GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.6 Sol models. The models were used for language correction, translation, and simplifying complex sentences. The tools provided support with technical questions, including comparing literature, reviewing existing source code, and considering alternative implementations. The generated responses were not used without review. They were checked against the cited sources, the source code, the author's own results, and were revised where necessary. All decisions regarding the implementation, evaluation, and interpretation of the results were made by the author.

The use of generative AI during development, especially for source code, technical documentation, and illustrations, is documented separately in the repository README under \emph{Use of AI during development}.\footnote{Project repository and full AI declaration: \href{https://github.com/jannick1312/BCs-VR-Museum/blob/main/README.md}{README in the project repository}.} Figure~\ref{fig:concept-interaction-inputs} and the conceptual sketches in Figures~\ref{fig:concept-presentation-templates} and~\ref{fig:concept-placement-areas} were created with ChatGPT based on the author's content and visual instructions.

\endgroup
